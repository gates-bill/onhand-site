OnHand Support

Need help with OnHand? This page provides basic support information for the app.

Common help topics

OnHand includes tools for household food inventory, barcode scanning, shopping lists, recipes, meal planning, receipt scanning, and optional AI-powered grocery recognition.

AI and rewarded ads

Some AI features have usage limits. OnHand may offer an optional rewarded advertisement that grants an additional AI scan after the reward is successfully confirmed.

Privacy

For information about how OnHand handles data, read the Privacy Policy.

Contact
