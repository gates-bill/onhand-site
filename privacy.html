OnHand Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

OnHand is a household food inventory, shopping, recipe, meal-planning, and AI-assisted organization app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use OnHand.

Information stored on your device

Most of the information you enter into OnHand is stored locally on your device. This may include inventory items, quantities, locations, shopping-list entries, recipes, meal plans, purchase history, receipt details, and related notes.

AI-powered features

When you choose to use an AI-powered feature, such as grocery photo scanning, receipt scanning, or recipe import/analysis, the content you submit may be sent securely to service providers used to process the request. Depending on the feature, this may include photos, screenshots, recipe text, recipe URLs, receipt content, or other information you intentionally provide.

OnHand uses cloud services, including Supabase and OpenAI, to support these features. Submitted content is used to provide the requested result and to operate the feature. OnHand does not require you to create a traditional user account to use the app.

Anonymous installation identifiers and AI usage limits

OnHand may use an anonymous installation identifier to manage complimentary AI usage, rewarded-ad AI credits, abuse prevention, and related service operations. This identifier is not intended to directly identify you by name.

Advertising

OnHand may offer optional rewarded advertisements that you can choose to watch in exchange for an additional AI scan or similar in-app benefit. OnHand uses Google AdMob to provide these advertisements. Google may collect information as described in its own privacy practices, which can include device, advertising, diagnostic, and interaction information depending on your device, settings, region, and applicable consent requirements.

OnHand does not use forced interstitial advertisements or banner advertisements as part of its current launch model.

Barcode and product lookups

When you scan a barcode, OnHand may query third-party product databases, such as Open Food Facts, to retrieve product information. The barcode or product identifier may be sent to that service for lookup.

Recipe links and third-party content

If you provide a recipe URL or other third-party content, OnHand may access that content as necessary to provide the feature you requested. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to submit or use content you provide to OnHand.

Data sharing

OnHand does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to provide app functionality, process AI requests, deliver optional rewarded ads, maintain security, prevent abuse, and operate supporting infrastructure.

Data retention

Locally stored OnHand data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove the app's local data. Information processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to their applicable service terms, security practices, and legal obligations.

Children

OnHand is a general-audience household utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Your choices

You can choose whether to use AI-powered features and whether to watch rewarded advertisements. You can also manage certain privacy and advertising choices through your device settings and any consent controls presented by applicable third-party services.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data handled by OnHand and its service infrastructure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as OnHand changes. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are made.
